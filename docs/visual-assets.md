## Cinematic gateway illustration

Project asset: `public/art/azeroth-gateway.webp`.

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool, then encoded to WebP for delivery. This is a decorative fantasy illustration for the fan atlas, not official Blizzard artwork or evidence of a specific canonical location. The Three.js dust and armillary overlay are decorative, with a static artwork fallback and reduced-motion support.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ultra-wide cinematic website hero background for a Turkish World of Warcraft history atlas. Create exquisite high-fantasy environmental key art of Azeroth: a colossal ancient weathered stone gateway on a rugged hill on the RIGHT THIRD, looking through its arch toward a distant luminous blue-gold world tree and mountain kingdoms. A small cloaked traveler stands at the threshold, back to viewer, giving epic scale. Floating ruins and a few distant dragons in a vast misty sky, richly detailed Warcraft-like architecture, realistic matte-painting texture, artbook quality. LEFT HALF is dark atmospheric open space with subdued mist and very minimal detail for large cream editorial website typography to be added in code. Hero composition landscape 16:9 or wider, dramatic chiaroscuro, charcoal teal shadows, moonlit desaturated blue, restrained antique gold highlights, atmospheric depth, subtle grain, luxurious dark fantasy mood rather than bright game screenshot. Right architecture and tree highly legible even after dark overlay. No words, no letters, no logos, no interface, no border, no watermark. Intended as web illustration, not a slide or mockup.

Typography: self-hosted Cinzel and Manrope through Fontsource packages. Interface icons: Lucide. Source prose, tables, period labels and caveats remain from the supplied presentation; this adaptation does not independently revise or verify its lore and release-date claims.
